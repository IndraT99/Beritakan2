## 2024-05-15 - Duplicate dropdown IDs in header\n**Learning:** The header component contained multiple dropdown elements sharing the same `id="dropdownMenuButton1"`, which violates HTML validity and causes accessibility issues since `aria-labelledby` on the dropdown menus cannot uniquely identify their corresponding buttons.\n**Action:** Assign unique IDs to dropdown toggle buttons and their corresponding menus in Bootstrap components to ensure screen readers can accurately associate them.
