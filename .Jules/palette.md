## 2026-07-20 - Adding aria-label and fixing double focus on icon-only search toggle\n**Learning:** When adding `aria-label` to icon-only buttons that wrap an inner `<a>` element with an icon, blind screen reader users will experience double focus stops, or empty links, if the `<a>` element isn't hidden. \n**Action:** Apply `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabindex="-1"` to inner `<a>` elements when wrapping them inside an accessible button, or alternatively just put the `aria-label` on the `<a>` tag directly if there is no wrapper button.
